Next we will have to design the turn based combat. First off, I would like to define an action point system, and a move speed both defined based on the players agility stat. Lets start with action points and movement points equal to the characters agility stat, not the modifier. One movement point can equal one tile. Both will always reset to full for a given entity at the start of its own turn, unless they have a status effect applied or something. Ranged and melee attacks will both have a chance of hitting or missing. We have to come up with the exact formula for this. It will be based on the effective ranged or melee stat for the attacking character after applying relevant modifiers. It will also be negatively effected by the dodge stat of the character being attacked. Other situational modifiers can be added to the formula as needed, such as darkness or abilities making a character harder to hit. The final actual percentage chance to hit should be displayed to the user when they hover the option to confirm their attack against an enemy. The numbers on this will take a lot of tweaking, and we can start with some guesses at decent values to start. Every weapon will have a specific action point cost to attack with it, with lower action point costs for weapons that can be used quickly. Lets say an unarmed fist and the basic knife both cost 2 action points (AP) to use. Items in the inventory will also sometimes be usable in combat, such as a throwable weapon, or a healing item. These will also have corresponding action point costs. Characters will also have abilities that can be used for various AP costs and act similarly to attacks with weapons, consuming/thorwing an item, or some other effect. They will all have a cooldown, though for some the cooldown will be zero turns and they can be used multiple times in a turn. Items will also have cooldowns. Regular weapon attacks will never have a cooldown. Some weapons will have an ability the user will gain access to when they have that weapon equipped. For instance, the artificers starting blunderbuss will have a 'reload' ability, and after firing it will not be able to fire again until the reload ability is used. Lets make it cost 3 to fire and 2 to reload (all numbers are tentative though).
